RTT/2 Engine

Regime Mapping • Coherence Geometry • Drift Detection

Overview

RTT/2 is the second engine in the Resonance‑Time Theory (RTT) triad. It identifies the operational regime of an input, computes coherence geometry, and detects drift across structural, energetic, and resonant dimensions.

Core Functions

  • Regime Mapping — Classifies inputs into S‑Mode, E‑Mode, and R‑Mode.
  • Coherence Geometry — Computes alignment vectors, coherence surfaces, and stability gradients.
  • Drift Detection — Identifies structural, energetic, resonant, and cross‑regime drift.

Pipeline Position

RTT/2 sits between RTT/1 and RTT/3, feeding RTT/12 for full‑pipeline aggregation. 

RTT/1 → RTT/2 → RTT/3 → RTT/12

Repository Structure

  • /engine/ — Regime map, coherence geometry, drift scan, topology.
  • /api/ — Map + Regime endpoints (client, worker, schema, OpenAPI).
  • /docs/ — Protocols and integration notes.
  • /src/ — Engine implementations and tests.
  • /assets/ — CSS, JS, and OG images.
